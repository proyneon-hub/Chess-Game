# V5 tuning and corrections

Initial config: `2026-09-10.1`. The encounter coefficients are immutable values
attached to this config in the registry. No historical config is modified.

No extra refusal, heroism or assassination-success lottery increase has been
introduced. Withdrawal uses the specification's new 3–6% eligible interval.

Development corrections (not disguised as passing cohorts):

1. Preserve v4 intended/actual attribution on the new schema; retain hazards.
2. Replace the weaker passive mover fear increment with +4 for a complete actual
   danger interval, after grace, and prevent immediate calm cancellation.
3. Record actual support allocation in harmful-action references. Do not require
   the same sole defender on both harmful actions in the new route.
4. Permit a complaint supported by an involved subject; do not require both
   participants to have independently suffered two harms.
5. Validate a two-step separation path instead of demanding a one-move resolution.
6. Require a loyal court for renewed supportive-court follow-ups, so a previous
   fulfilled request cannot crowd out a subsequently established harmful arc.
7. Count distinct grave action revisions for v5 conspiracy eligibility; a single
   action with multiple deltas is never multiple grievances.
8. Keep inherited friction factual but open its v5 consequence as a dispute
   request; do not silently subtract the v4 friction score in v5.
9. Require fresh causal treatment for a new dispute after the earlier one closes.
10. Report modifier creation, applicability and actual forecast changes separately.

Interpretation to review: a self-rescue without a separate helper grants steady
cooperation, while an actual helper grants a one-use support token. This supplies
an immediate bounded consumer without duplicating the old rescue stat reward.

The inherited pressure policy deliberately rescues its target once resentment
grows. `sustained-pressure` is a separately labeled legal-command policy that
continues mistreatment; its opponent remains board-only ordinary. Cooperative
replays are never included in these pressure denominators.

11. Protection fulfillment must reduce danger during this actual action; a threat
    already removed by an opponent cannot reward a later unrelated move.
12. Read recent opening activity from factual move events for confidence eligibility,
    without adding opening fatigue or queued mistreatment.
13. Measurement v3 uses separate actor RNG streams (2*seed+1 and 2*seed+2) and
    swaps their colors. Earlier symmetric cohorts duplicated their paired games;
    they are diagnostic snapshots, not definitive independent evidence.
14. Relationship/supportive arc checkpoint metrics now require offers by ply 64;
    the initial harness accidentally included later offers in those two fields.

15. Opening guarantees include attempts made while the completed-ply clock reads
    eight, preventing a refusal from revealing agency before the ninth move.
16. Withdrawal alternatives compare with capture-adjusted intended residual loss,
    not the gross value exposed at the destination. Historical forecasts retain
    their recorded implementation.
17. V5 unresolved disputes use the temporary +0.04 modifier without stacking the
    old relationship dispute contribution. Rival wording follows the applicable
    temporary modifier. Check extends only steady's eligible-turn duration;
    support and dispute modifiers retain their six-own-turn maximum.
18. Protection/relief bonuses remember attacker identities or the relieved wards
    for 32 own turns in the existing bounded memory. A repeated threat can close
    a request but cannot mint another token during that interval. This is an
    explicit anti-farming tuning choice; pair cooldown remains six own turns.
19. Qualified strain is available in the first-personality phase as specified;
    ordinary protection remains locked until relationship phase.
20. Candidate priority is compared across both sides before side rotation. A
    routine opportunity cannot win merely because its side waited longer than
    a side with a genuine higher-priority problem. Regression covers this case.
21. Measurement v4 excludes fulfilled-but-unrewarded requests from mechanical
    resolution counts. Earlier reports preserve their original broader count.

Freeze 7 retains the original specified coefficients and odds. Freeze 6 jobs
were interrupted before holdouts, and are not passed cohorts. Independent
holdouts are a candid evaluation of the frozen implementation even where
development gates remain unmet, not release acceptance by implication.
